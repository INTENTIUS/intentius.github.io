Every infrastructure tool outside Kubernetes keeps a private ledger of what it owns, and that ledger is where the permission boundary, the secrets and the lock end up. Both tools here put the record where IAM can read it instead.

[choudoufu](https://intentius.io/choudoufu/) is the smallest change to OpenTofu that does this for AWS. [chant](https://intentius.io/chant/) goes the other way, into the platform's own spec. Neither has a pricing page.

Every claim on this site has a test behind it, or a public issue admitting it is not done yet. Bugs found in other people's projects get fixed there rather than worked around here.

The thinking is on [the blog](https://lex00.github.io/).
