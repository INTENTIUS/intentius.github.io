Every infrastructure tool outside Kubernetes keeps a private ledger of what it owns, and that ledger is where the permission boundary, the secrets and the lock end up. Both tools here put the record where IAM can read it instead.

[choudoufu](https://intentius.io/choudoufu/) is the smallest change to OpenTofu that does this for AWS. [chant](https://intentius.io/chant/) goes the other way, out of Terraform and into the platform's own spec. Neither has a pricing page.

## How the work goes

Measured, not argued. A claim about either tool links to the test that proves it or the issue that says it is not done. Changes to other people's projects go upstream as reviewable stacks with tests.

The thinking is on [the blog](https://lex00.github.io/).
