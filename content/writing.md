Everything Intentius argues on this site was argued first, at more length and with more heat, on [the founder's blog](https://lex00.github.io/). It is one person's blog and it reads like one. Start here.

- [Terraform state has three pieces](https://lex00.github.io/posts/terraform-state-has-three-pieces/). A state file does three jobs. Bundling them turns persistence into a permission boundary and a lock.
- [Invisible ownership is ridiculous](https://lex00.github.io/posts/invisible-ownership-is-ridiculous/). Kubernetes tracks which manager owns each field. Terraform does not think about ownership at all.
- [Infra tooling expectations in 2026](https://lex00.github.io/posts/infra-tooling-expectations-in-2026/). Tooling should ship with CI and a control plane, work with your forge, and run on your own infra. Ask a vendor what stops someone using the CLI.
- [Adopt infrastructure without re-homing it](https://lex00.github.io/posts/adopt-without-rehoming/). Two ways to inherit a Terraform estate, and neither keeps a separate ownership record.
- [The intoxicating mix of synthesis and execution](https://lex00.github.io/posts/the-intoxicating-mix-of-synthesis-and-execution/). Why chant refuses to run your program.
- [Terraform state at scale the easy way](https://lex00.github.io/posts/terraform-state-at-scale-the-easy-way/). An RDBMS for state against the live backend, on five concerns and on price.
- [Take a whiff of choudoufu](https://lex00.github.io/posts/take-a-whiff-of-choudoufu/). The introduction, and the name.
