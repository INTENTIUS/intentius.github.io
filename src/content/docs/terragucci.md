---
title: "terragucci"
description: "Plan, approve and apply from pull requests, in your own CI."
---

terragucci runs Terraform, OpenTofu and Terragrunt as jobs in your own CI. State, plans and approvals stay in your git and your bucket. It is open source under Apache-2.0.

```bash
npm i -D @intentius/terragucci
npx terragucci init
```

[Docs](https://intentius.io/terragucci/) · [Tutorial](https://intentius.io/terragucci/tutorial/) · [Source](https://github.com/INTENTIUS/terragucci)

If you would rather not set it up alone, [I can help](/work/#rollout).
